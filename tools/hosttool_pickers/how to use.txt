Requires Python 3 on PATH.

1. Open generate_pickers.bat in a text editor.
2. Set MOD to the SPT folder containing descriptor.mod, and GAME to the HOI4 folder containing hoi4.exe.
3. Save it and double-click the BAT file.

The generator reads SPT's focus trees and ideas, plus the vanilla files SPT uses. It overwrites the focus and spirit picker decision files and their English localization in your SPT folder.

Regenerate after changing focuses or ideas, or updating HOI4. Restart HOI4 to load the new lists.
